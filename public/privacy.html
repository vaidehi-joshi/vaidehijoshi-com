Privacy Policy

Video Generator · Last updated: October 2026

Video Generator is a personal tool operated by Vaidehi Joshi (vaidehijoshi.com). It creates short videos and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

When the operator signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to upload videos to, and manage captions on, the operator's own YouTube channel (the YouTube upload and YouTube force-ssl scopes). It uses these permissions only to upload the videos and caption files it creates.

What the app stores

The sign-in token is stored in a file on the operator's own computer. It is not sent to any server or third party. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone else.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
